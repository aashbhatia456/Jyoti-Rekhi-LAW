Attorney Profile

Jyoti Rekhi

Jyoti Rekhi is a criminal defense attorney with extensive experience representing clients in state and federal court. Before founding her current practice, she spent more than fourteen years with Cardoza Law Offices and previously served as a Deputy Public Defender in Riverside, California.

Her background includes years of criminal litigation, negotiation, oral advocacy, and misdemeanor trial work. Her practice is centered on giving clients direct communication, careful case analysis, and a defense strategy tailored to the facts of each matter.
