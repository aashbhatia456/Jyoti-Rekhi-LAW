Meet The Attorney

Experience backed by personal attention.

Jyoti Rekhi earned her undergraduate degree in English Language & Literature from UC Irvine and her Juris Doctor from Santa Clara University School of Law in 2004.

Her career includes service as a Deputy Public Defender in Riverside and more than fourteen years with Cardoza Law Offices, where her work included complex criminal litigation in state and federal court.

Today, her practice combines that experience with direct, individualized representation for clients facing serious criminal allegations.
