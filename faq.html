FAQs

Clear answers to common questions.

When should I contact a criminal defense attorney?

As early as possible if you have been arrested, cited, contacted by law enforcement, or believe you are under investigation.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Any citation, charging document, court notice, police paperwork, bail information, deadlines, and a basic timeline of what happened.

Should I talk to law enforcement before speaking with a lawyer?

Because the right decision depends on the circumstances, it is generally wise to understand your rights and legal position before giving a detailed statement.

Does contacting the firm create an attorney-client relationship?

No. Representation begins only after the firm agrees to take the matter and the required engagement process is completed.
