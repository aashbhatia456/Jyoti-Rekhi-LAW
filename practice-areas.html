Criminal Defense

Alcohol-related offenses

Alcohol-related cases can create both criminal and driving-related consequences, with deadlines that may arrive quickly.

What to do now: Keep every citation and court or DMV document, write down what you remember, and avoid discussing the facts publicly.
How Jyoti can help: Review the stop, testing, statements, deadlines, and available evidence; explain potential consequences and develop a defense strategy.

Assault and battery

These allegations often depend on competing accounts, injuries, video, witness testimony, and possible self-defense issues.

What to do now: Preserve messages, photos, videos, and witness information. Avoid contact that could escalate the situation or violate an order.
How Jyoti can help: Evaluate self-defense and factual disputes, examine witness and physical evidence, and advocate through negotiation or litigation.

Child abuse

Allegations involving a child can carry serious criminal and personal consequences and may involve multiple agencies or proceedings.

What to do now: Do not confront witnesses or attempt to influence statements. Preserve relevant records and comply carefully with any court orders.
How Jyoti can help: Assess the allegations and evidence, explain overlapping proceedings, protect your rights, and develop a careful case strategy.

Disorderly conduct

Disorderly conduct cases can arise from public incidents, disputes, or conduct law enforcement believes disturbed the peace.

What to do now: Save videos, witness names, citations, and other records showing the context of the incident.
How Jyoti can help: Review whether the alleged conduct meets the legal elements, evaluate witness accounts, and pursue an appropriate resolution.

Extortion

Extortion allegations often turn on communications, threats, intent, and the surrounding relationship between the parties.

What to do now: Preserve texts, emails, recordings, and financial records. Do not delete or alter communications.
How Jyoti can help: Analyze the communications and context, evaluate intent and evidentiary issues, and build a defense around what the evidence actually establishes.

Fraud

Fraud matters can involve detailed financial records, representations, transactions, and questions about intent.

What to do now: Preserve contracts, messages, receipts, account records, and other documentation. Avoid trying to explain the matter to investigators without counsel.
How Jyoti can help: Organize and analyze the documentary evidence, examine intent and knowledge, and challenge unsupported inferences.

Identity theft

Identity theft allegations may involve digital records, financial accounts, devices, and questions about who accessed or used information.

What to do now: Preserve account records and communications and do not delete electronic information that may be relevant.
How Jyoti can help: Examine digital and financial evidence, identification issues, access records, and what the prosecution must prove.
Criminal Defense

Money laundering

These cases can involve complex financial transactions and allegations about the source, movement, or purpose of funds.

What to do now: Preserve financial and business records and avoid altering accounts or records connected to the investigation.
How Jyoti can help: Analyze transaction history, knowledge and intent issues, and the government's financial evidence.

Murder and manslaughter

Homicide allegations are among the most serious criminal charges and require immediate, careful legal representation.

What to do now: Do not discuss the facts with investigators, witnesses, or on social media. Preserve potential evidence and seek counsel immediately.
How Jyoti can help: Conduct detailed case review, scrutinize forensic and witness evidence, evaluate defenses, and provide intensive representation through every stage.

Rape

Sex offense allegations can carry severe criminal, registration, and reputational consequences.

What to do now: Do not contact the accusing party or discuss the allegation publicly. Preserve communications and other relevant records.
How Jyoti can help: Carefully review statements, communications, forensic evidence, consent issues where applicable, and inconsistencies in the evidence.
Criminal Defense

Sexual assault

Sexual assault cases often involve highly sensitive allegations and evidence requiring discreet, detailed review.

What to do now: Preserve messages and records, avoid contact with involved parties, and follow every court order exactly.
How Jyoti can help: Provide confidential guidance, examine the evidence and timeline, and build a defense responsive to the specific allegations.

Stalking

Stalking allegations may involve repeated communications, location evidence, social media, and protective orders.

What to do now: Stop any contact that could violate an order or worsen the allegations. Preserve communications and location records.
How Jyoti can help: Review the alleged pattern of conduct, communications, intent, and protective-order issues.
Criminal Defense

Arson

Arson investigations may rely on fire-scene analysis, expert opinions, surveillance, motive allegations, and witness statements.

What to do now: Do not disturb or destroy potentially relevant records or evidence. Preserve communications and location information.
How Jyoti can help: Evaluate forensic conclusions, expert evidence, identification, motive, and whether the evidence supports the charged offense.
Criminal Defense

Burglary

Burglary cases can turn on entry, intent, identification, possession of property, and surveillance evidence.

What to do now: Preserve location records, communications, receipts, and other evidence that may establish where you were or what occurred.
How Jyoti can help: Examine identification and intent evidence, surveillance, witness accounts, and the prosecution's theory of entry.

Cyber crime

Cybercrime allegations may involve devices, accounts, IP data, digital communications, and complex technical evidence.

What to do now: Do not delete accounts, messages, files, or device data. Preserve notices and records related to the investigation.
How Jyoti can help: Analyze digital evidence and attribution issues, challenge assumptions about account or device use, and coordinate technical review where appropriate.

Drug offenses

Drug cases often involve search-and-seizure questions, possession, knowledge, quantity, and alleged intent.

What to do now: Keep all paperwork and note how any search occurred. Avoid additional statements about ownership, possession, or intent.
How Jyoti can help: Review the legality of searches, possession and intent issues, laboratory evidence, and available resolutions.
Criminal Defense

Forgery

Forgery allegations may involve signatures, documents, checks, electronic records, or questions about authorization and intent.

What to do now: Preserve original documents and communications and avoid modifying potentially relevant records.
How Jyoti can help: Review authenticity, authorization, handwriting or digital evidence, and whether intent can actually be established.
Criminal Defense

Hate crimes

Hate-crime allegations can add serious consequences to an underlying offense and often focus on alleged motive or bias.

What to do now: Preserve the full context of communications and the incident. Do not post about the case online.
How Jyoti can help: Evaluate the underlying charge and separately scrutinize the evidence offered to prove prohibited motive or bias.

Larceny and theft

Theft cases often depend on ownership, possession, identification, value, and intent to deprive another person of property.

What to do now: Save receipts, messages, transaction records, and any evidence explaining possession or ownership.
How Jyoti can help: Challenge identification, value, intent, and possession evidence and evaluate the strongest path toward resolution.

Motor vehicle theft

Vehicle theft allegations can involve possession, identification, keys, location data, and knowledge that a vehicle was stolen.

What to do now: Preserve location records, communications, receipts, and information showing how you came into contact with the vehicle.
How Jyoti can help: Analyze knowledge, possession, identification, and digital or physical evidence connecting a person to the vehicle.

Offenses against family

Family-related criminal allegations can overlap with protective orders, custody issues, and restrictions on contact.

What to do now: Follow all court orders exactly, even if another person initiates contact. Save relevant communications.
How Jyoti can help: Explain criminal and protective-order consequences, evaluate witness evidence, and help avoid additional legal exposure.

Robbery

Robbery charges can involve identification, force or fear, possession of property, surveillance, and eyewitness testimony.

What to do now: Preserve location and communication records and avoid discussing the incident with potential witnesses.
How Jyoti can help: Examine identification procedures, surveillance, witness reliability, possession evidence, and whether each element can be proven.
Criminal Defense

Solicitation

Solicitation allegations can depend heavily on communications, intent, and what was allegedly requested or agreed to.

What to do now: Preserve all messages and records and do not attempt to alter or explain communications after the fact.
How Jyoti can help: Review the exact communications and context, evaluate intent and entrapment-related issues where relevant, and challenge unsupported interpretations.
Criminal Defense

Vandalism

Vandalism cases can involve property damage, identification, repair estimates, video, and disputes over intent.

What to do now: Photograph relevant conditions when lawful, preserve receipts or communications, and save any location or witness evidence.
How Jyoti can help: Review identification, intent, ownership, and damage valuation and pursue an appropriate defense or resolution.
