Practice Areas

Know what to do next.

General educational guidance for common criminal defense situations. This is not legal advice for any specific case.

DUI & Alcohol-Related Offenses

DUI and alcohol-related charges

These cases can involve both criminal court consequences and separate driving-related deadlines. The facts surrounding the stop, testing, statements, and field sobriety procedures may all matter.

What to do now: Save every document you received, write down what you remember while it is fresh, note any upcoming DMV or court deadlines, and avoid discussing the facts publicly or on social media.
How Jyoti can help: Review the stop and testing procedures, identify deadlines, explain possible defenses and consequences, communicate with prosecutors, and guide you through court proceedings.
Assault & Battery

Assault and battery allegations

These cases can depend heavily on witness accounts, video, injuries, self-defense issues, and the sequence of events.

What to do now: Preserve texts, photos, videos, names of witnesses, and any evidence showing what happened. Avoid contacting the accusing party if there is any court order or risk of escalating the situation.
How Jyoti can help: Evaluate self-defense or factual disputes, review available evidence, identify weaknesses in the allegations, and develop a strategy for negotiation or litigation.
Theft, Burglary & Fraud

Theft, burglary, fraud, and identity-related cases

These matters may involve documents, electronic records, financial information, surveillance, or questions about intent and ownership.

What to do now: Do not delete messages, receipts, records, or account information. Save relevant documents and avoid trying to “explain” the situation to investigators without legal advice.
How Jyoti can help: Analyze what the prosecution must prove, review documentary evidence, assess intent and identification issues, and advocate for the strongest available resolution.
Drug Offenses

Drug possession and related charges

Drug cases often turn on search-and-seizure issues, possession, knowledge, intent, and how law enforcement obtained evidence.

What to do now: Keep all paperwork, note where and how any search occurred, and avoid making additional statements about ownership, possession, or intent.
How Jyoti can help: Review whether evidence was lawfully obtained, assess possession and intent issues, explain available options, and handle negotiations and court appearances.
Family & Domestic Allegations

Offenses involving family or domestic relationships

These cases can involve criminal charges, protective orders, family dynamics, and restrictions on contact.

What to do now: Follow every court order exactly, even if the other person contacts you first. Save relevant communications and avoid social media posts about the case.
How Jyoti can help: Explain the criminal process, review protective-order implications, evaluate evidence and witness statements, and help prevent avoidable mistakes while the case is pending.
Additional Criminal Matters

Other matters the firm may handle

Additional areas include child abuse, disorderly conduct, extortion, money laundering, murder and manslaughter, sexual offenses, stalking, arson, cyber crime, forgery, hate crimes, motor vehicle theft, robbery, solicitation, and vandalism.

What to do now: Preserve documents and evidence, comply with all court orders, track every deadline, and avoid making statements about the incident until you understand your rights and options.
How Jyoti can help: Evaluate the allegations, explain the process and potential consequences, identify defenses and evidentiary issues, and build a case-specific strategy.
